Getting Started

Not an app download.
A web app you connect.

Spark runs as a Web App directly on your Ray-Ban Display Glasses — added in seconds through the Meta AI app. No App Store. No download.

1
Get the Meta AI App
Download Meta AI from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with your Meta account. Make sure your Ray-Ban Display Glasses are paired.
2
Enable Developer Mode
In the Meta AI app go to Settings → App Info, then tap the version number five times. Tap Enable when prompted. This unlocks Web App support.
3
Add Spark as a Web App
Go to App Settings → App Connections → Web Apps → Add a Web App. Enter Spark as the name and smartsparks.app as the URL. Tap Connect.
4
Put on your glasses and launch
Spark appears in your glasses apps grid under Web Apps. Select it with the D-pad — messages, rooms, and translation appear right in your display.
