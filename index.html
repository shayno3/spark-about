✦ Questions, answered

Before you connect.

The six things everyone asks first — straight answers, no fine print.

How do I get Spark? I can't find it in the App Store.

You don't download Spark — it's a web app you connect, so there's no App Store or Google Play listing.

  1. Set up on your phone first — go to smartsparks.app, sign up, and create your profile.
  2. Connect it to your glasses — in the Meta AI app, go to Developer Mode → Web Apps → Add “Spark”.
  3. Unlock on your glasses — enter your Glasses Code, then your PIN.

On your phone, you can also add Spark to your Home Screen from your browser and use it like an app.

Do I need the glasses to use Spark?

No. Spark works on your phone's browser on its own — join, match, and chat from your phone. The Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses add the hands-free experience: profiles and messages right in your lens, speak-to-type, and messages read aloud. Same account, same conversations, on both.

How much does Spark cost?

Spark itself is free — Discover, matching, and messaging, with 8 likes and 1 Super Like a day.

Spark Premium is $9.99/month and covers both the app and the Lava Lounge: unlimited likes, 6 profile photos, see who liked and viewed you, translation in the user's preferred language, and full Lounge access. Cancel any time through Stripe, in Profile → Manage Subscription.

What is Founder's Access?

New members get 30 days of Premium free — that's Founder's Access. Everything Premium includes, free for your first 30 days. When it ends, Spark stays free; the Premium features simply lock unless you subscribe. Your Profile shows your Founder's Access countdown.

Is date check-in and safety really free?

Yes — every safety feature is free for every member. Before a date, log who you're meeting, where, and when. Spark checks in on you at the time you choose; tap “I'm safe,” or “I need help” to send your live location to a friend in one tap, with a Call 911 option. You can also share your date plan with a friend who doesn't use Spark. Date safe. It's free.

What is the Lava Lounge?

The Lava Lounge is Spark's live group chat — 9 themed rooms (Friday Night Vibes, Music & Moods, Wanderlust, Foodies Only, Ambitious Minds, Sports Talk, a men-only Man Cave, a women-only Safe Place, and Fact or Fake?), plus event pop-up rooms, trivia nights, and games. Get to know people's personalities first, then tap anyone to see their profile and like them in Spark. The Lounge is part of Premium — and it's included in your 30 days of Founder's Access.
